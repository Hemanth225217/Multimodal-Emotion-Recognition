Key findings

  • The fusion relies almost entirely on text; much stronger WavLM audio received less weight.
  • On MELD (multi-party) the models ignore the voices and faces of the surrounding lines.
  • On MC-EIU (two-person) they use context, above all the partner's earlier words.
  • Scene video carried no emotion signal; face-expression features make video useful.
  • Context dropout gives a statistically confirmed gain on MC-EIU.
  • When all ensemble members agree, the prediction is right far more often (reliability signal).
